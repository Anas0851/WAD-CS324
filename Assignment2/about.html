About us

PantryPal started with a simple problem: a few ingredients in the kitchen and no plan for dinner.

It is a recipe library with an AI cooking assistant built in. You can browse and filter recipes like on any food site, or type in what you have and get meals you can make with it. You can then ask for changes, like cooking without an oven or making a dish less spicy.

We made it mainly for university students and beginners who cook on a small budget with limited equipment. It should also help anyone who wants to waste less food.
