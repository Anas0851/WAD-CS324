Egg fried rice

A good way to use up leftover rice and whatever vegetables are in the freezer.

  • Time: 20 min
  • Difficulty: Easy
  • Serves: 2

Steps

  1. Heat one tablespoon of oil in a wide pan over medium-high heat. Cook the carrot for two minutes, then add the peas for another minute. Move them to a plate.
  2. Add the rest of the oil. Crack in the eggs, season with a pinch of salt and scramble until just set.
  3. Tip the rice into the pan and break up any clumps. Fry for three to four minutes until it is hot and starting to crisp.
  4. Put the vegetables back in the pan, pour in the soy sauce and toss everything together.
  5. Take the pan off the heat and stir in the spring onions. Taste, then add pepper or more soy sauce if it needs it.
